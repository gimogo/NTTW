NOTES To The World: an on-chain corkboard where every sticky note is an ERC-721 and its prize pool is settled cross-chain by Chainlink VRF v2.5.
A stamp costs 1000 NOTES: 500 burned, 500 pooled; stamps are epoch tickets (3 days on production). Anyone can settle at epoch end for the CCIP fee; the ownerless VrfCcipRelay (Base) requests one VRF word and returns it; the wall computes winner = word % ticketCount and pays via claimReward().
Custom: IMDRetroWall ERC-721 wall (Robinhood 4663), VrfCcipRelay (Base). NOTES launched via the IMD Protocol as a Uniswap V4 NOTES/IMD pool; its guard hook is IMD's, not ours.
Honest: the first production relay lacked ERC-165, so CCIP silenced its messages; redeployed 2026-10-16, ERC-165 verified on-chain. CCIP wall-to-relay ran live on mainnets (test-wall rehearsal); VRF pending on Chainlink's side - no WinnerDrawn yet. IMD audit covered the earlier leaderboard design (d063100); v3 not re-audited.
